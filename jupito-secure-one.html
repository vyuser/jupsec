SECURITY MODEL

SECURITY FOLLOWS THE EXECUTION.

The same control sequence follows users, applications, workspaces, browsers, private resources and AI execution.

01 IDENTITY Who is requesting access?

Establish identity and context for the user, device, application or AI agent.

02 POLICY What is permitted?

Apply organizational, resource, workspace, browser or AI execution policies.

03 DLP What data can move?

Control data movement and protect sensitive information.

04 EXECUTION Where does the work happen?

Execute through the appropriate workspace, browser, isolated environment or controlled AI runtime.

05 GOVERNANCE What controls apply?

Enforce organizational requirements throughout the execution lifecycle.

06 AUDIT What happened?

Record relevant access, policy, execution and security events.

AI SECURITY EXTENSION

AI execution is governed before it reaches the tool or resource.

The AI platform adds rights, approvals, data entitlements and tool permissions to the execution decision.

Prompt→ Context→ Data→ Tool→ Agent→ Execution→ Response
AI request ≠ automatic trust. Rights, approvals, data entitlements and tool permissions participate in the decision.
