5.4 ZERO INBOUND PORTS

Make Inbound Exposure the Exception — Not the Foundation

Traditional remote access frequently begins by exposing a reachable service. TeleConnect reverses the connectivity model.

TRADITIONAL EXPOSURE

Internet → Inbound Port → Protected Network

Connectivity begins with an externally reachable service.

TELECONNECT

Protected Environment → Outbound Connection → TeleConnect → Authorized Resource

Connectivity begins from the protected environment.

Security Principle

Connect out. Do not expose in by default.
